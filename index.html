About JMB Services

Your Trusted Partner for Batching Plant & RMC Plant Solutions

Jai Maa Bhavani Services provides professional solutions and technical support for concrete batching plants and RMC plants. Our focus is on reliable equipment support, efficient plant operations, maintenance and customer-focused service.

We help construction companies, infrastructure contractors and RMC plant operators maintain efficient, reliable and productive concrete operations — from preventive batching plant maintenance to breakdown repair, electrical & control systems and spare part support.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about our batching plant and RMC plant services.

Which plants does Jai Maa Bhavani Services support?
We provide solutions, maintenance, repair and technical support for concrete batching plants and RMC (ready-mix concrete) plants — including static and mobile plants, cement silos, mixers, conveyors and related plant systems.
Do you provide both maintenance and repair services?
Yes. We offer preventive maintenance, corrective maintenance, breakdown repair and troubleshooting support — all focused on keeping batching plant and RMC plant operations efficient and dependable.
Can you help with electrical, control and automation issues?
Yes. Our services cover electrical systems, control panels, automation and plant controls, alongside mechanical and structural plant support. Share your issue and we will guide you on the right approach.
Do you supply spare parts for batching plants?
We provide assistance with suitable spare parts and replacement requirements for batching plant and RMC plant equipment. Contact us with your requirement and we will help identify a suitable option.
How can I request a service or a quotation?

Which areas do you serve?
We are based in Daltonganj, Palamu, Jharkhand and support batching plant and RMC plant operators in and around the region. Contact us to discuss service for your specific plant location.
Do you offer long-term service arrangements?
Yes. Many plant operators rely on us for ongoing technical assistance, scheduled service and dependable support. We are happy to discuss a service arrangement that fits your plant's needs.
